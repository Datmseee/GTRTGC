Sengkang–Punggol LRT — Mileage & PM Tracking
Quick Start

This dashboard tracks 59 trains across 4 loops. Click any train on the map or in the fleet list to view details.

Green trains are OK. Amber means PM due soon. Red means PM overdue — schedule maintenance immediately.

PM Thresholds
  • 2K — 2,000 km: Visual inspection
  • 13K — 13,000 km: Function checks (doors)
  • 40K — 40,000 km: Extended checks (air-con, brakes)
  • 120K — 120,000 km: Detail checks (greasing)
  • 360K — 360,000 km: Full overhaul
